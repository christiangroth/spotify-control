Playback Settings

Playback Data

Rebuild all processed playback data (app_playback and app_track_data) from scratch by reprocessing the raw Spotify data. Use this if your playback stats appear incomplete or outdated.

Missing Artists

Sync artists that appear in your playback history but are not yet in the catalog, without re-syncing already known artists or rebuilding playback data.

Playback Aggregations

Delete all computed playback aggregations (day/week/month/quarter/year) and recompute them from scratch. Use this if stats look outdated or incomplete.

User Profile

Reload your Spotify display name.

Playback Polling

Fetch currently playing and recently played data from Spotify right now, instead of waiting for the next scheduled poll.
